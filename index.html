The studio

Images with a reason to exist.

Lumacrom is Octavio Nessi’s independent creative studio for stories and visual worlds. Direction, CGI, animation and emerging image tools come together in service of an idea — not as a style in search of one.

From a world built from scratch to a collaboration with an artist or brand, the work begins by asking what the audience should feel, remember, and want to see next.
