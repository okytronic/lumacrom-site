The studio

Made to feel like something.

Lumacrom is an independent creative studio for cinematic image-making. We bring direction, CGI craft and emerging tools into one considered visual process.

From a single striking image to a complete campaign world, every frame starts with an idea worth seeing through.

01 / DIRECTION

Creative direction

Concept development, visual language and shot design for memorable moving images.

02 / IMAGE

CGI & animation

03 / PRODUCTION

AI-assisted films

New production workflows shaped by human direction, iteration and edit judgment.
